Aethera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Aethera ("we", "us") is operated by Seitkaziyeva Madina, Almaty, Kazakhstan. Contact: aether.appp@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account data. Email address and name, if you provide them, and sign-in data from Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Profile data. Date of birth (used only to confirm you are an adult), and your reminder preferences.
  • Content you create. Goals, affirmations and questions you choose or write, your answers in the personal questionnaire, and sessions ("efirs") you save.
  • Voice recordings. Recordings of your voice that you make inside the app. These are biometric-type data. We use them only to build your sessions and, if you choose, to create a synthetic copy of your voice. See section 4.
  • Purchase data. Subscription status and purchase identifiers, processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your card details.
  • Technical data. Device type, operating system version, app version and crash reports needed to keep the app working.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: build and play your sessions, sync them across your devices, and send reminders you turned on.
  • To generate affirmations and questions from the answers you give. These requests are sent to our AI provider (see section 5).
  • To manage your subscription and restore purchases.
  • To keep the app secure and fix bugs.

3. Legal basis (for EU/UK users)

We process data because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract), and for voice data and optional features, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Voice recordings and voice cloning

  • Recordings are stored in encrypted storage linked to your account.
  • If you choose to create a synthetic copy of your voice, we send your recordings to our voice-synthesis provider ([PROVIDER NAME]) and ask you to confirm that the voice is yours. We do not create a copy of anyone else's voice.
  • You can delete individual recordings, or delete your voice copy, at any time in the app. Deletion from our servers happens within 30 days, and the provider's copies are deleted under our contract with them.
  • We do not use your voice to train AI models for other users.

5. Service providers

We share data only with providers who need it to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage ([REGION]).
  • Anthropic: generates affirmations from your answers. We send only the text needed for the request.
  • [VOICE PROVIDER, if used]: voice synthesis, only for recordings and text you approved.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: purchases and subscriptions.
  • [CRASH REPORTING PROVIDER, if used].

6. Retention

  • Account and content data: kept while your account is active.
  • Voice recordings: kept until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • After account deletion, we delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it (for example, purchase records).

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. To delete your account, use "Delete account" in the app or email aether.appp@gmail.com. EU and UK users may also lodge a complaint with their data protection authority. California residents have the rights set out in the CCPA.

8. Children

Aethera is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact aether.appp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and row-level security in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password or Sign in with Apple.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

11. Changes

We will notify you in the app before material changes take effect.
